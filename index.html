aia-backup

A private, personal backup tool used by one person. It copies its owner's own files, encrypted on their computer before they leave it, into a folder of the owner's Google Drive. It is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

aia-backup asks Google Drive only for access to the files it creates itself (the drive.file scope). It cannot see, read or change any other file in the Drive.

Everything it uploads is encrypted before upload; Google stores only encrypted data. It collects no data about anyone, sends nothing to any other service, uses no analytics and has no other users.

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
